#### Lemma 2.4
[\[OAI26\], Lemma 3.2](bibligraphy.md/)

_The algebra $D(\ell, b)$ are free of rank $p^{n\ell-b}$ over $A\_m$. Moreover, the construction of this subalgebra is independent of the choice of coordinate, i.e. suppose we have a change of coordinate $\alpha: A\_m\[\[T\]\]\to A\_m\[\[\alpha(T)\]\]$, the Frobenius and hence $D(\ell, b)$ are unchanged.
